The Psychology Behind Viral Videos

Several psychological factors compel viewers to share videos, contributing to their virality. Emotionally charged content, whether it elicits laughter, empathy, or surprise, tends to perform better in attracting shares and interactions.

The concept of social validation also influences virality. People tend to share content that they feel reflects their beliefs or social status, enhancing their personal identity through association with certain media.

Lastly, the phenomenon of 'FOMO' (Fear of Missing Out) drives viewers to share content quickly. Videos that create a buzz, or that people feel they need to see and share, tend to achieve higher virality scores as they spread rapidly across social networks.
