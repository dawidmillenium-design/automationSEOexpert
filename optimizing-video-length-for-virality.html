Duration experiments worth running

Length is one of the easiest variables to A/B honestly. Take a working format and cut three versions — 15, 30 and 60 seconds — same hook, same payoff, differing only in how much supporting material you keep. Compare completion percentage and absolute watch time together: platforms weigh both, and the crossover point where extra length stops earning its retention cost is your format's true ideal duration. Re-test quarterly, because attention norms drift faster than production habits; the sweet spot you found last year is a hypothesis, not a fact.

Segment length inside long-form videos

Even when total runtime is fixed, internal structure changes retention math. Front-load the promised payoff within the first third, then alternate intensity — high-energy demonstration, calmer explanation, high-energy again — because continuous intensity exhausts mobile viewers and continuous calm loses them. Use chapters or on-screen progress markers so viewers self-select segments worth finishing; partial watches of relevant segments still beat full watches of irrelevant ones on most platform quality models.
