Using Video Length to Boost Virality

The length of a video can impact its virality score significantly. Shorter videos often perform better due to modern viewers' limited attention spans, making them more digestible.

However, longer videos can also perform well if they provide in-depth content, storytelling, or value that captivates viewers. The key is to balance length with engagement.

Testing different lengths can help creators find the sweet spot that resonates with their audience, ultimately leading to improved virality.
