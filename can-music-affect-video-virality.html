Can Music Affect Video Virality?

The music used in videos can significantly affect their virality score. Upbeat, catchy music tends to encourage shares and likes more than somber compositions.

Furthermore, leveraging popular tracks can align a video with trending sounds, boosting its visibility on platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Choosing the right soundtrack not only enhances viewer experience but can also create an emotional connection, encouraging viewers to share the content.
