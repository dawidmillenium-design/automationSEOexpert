The Science Behind Video Virality Score

The science behind video virality scores relies on analyzing viewer behavior and engagement patterns. Understanding how audiences interact with content allows for the prediction of viral potential.

Factors like cognitive load—the amount of mental effort needed to understand a video—can influence shares. Content that is easily digestible tends to have a higher virality score due to its accessibility.

Moreover, social validation, where people share content approved by others, can further bolster virality. By fostering engagement, creators can significantly enhance their video's overall score.
