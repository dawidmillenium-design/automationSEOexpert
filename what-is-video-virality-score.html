Understanding Video Virality Score

A video virality score measures how well a video is likely to perform in terms of engagement and shares across social platforms.

This score takes into account various factors such as viewer retention, shares, comments, and likes, essentially predicting a video's potential to go viral.

Understanding this score can help creators optimize their video content for better reach and engagement by focusing on what popular videos have in common.
