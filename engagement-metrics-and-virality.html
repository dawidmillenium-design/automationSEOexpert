Engagement Metrics and Their Impact on Video Virality

Engagement metrics such as likes, comments, and shares play a crucial role in determining a video's virality score.

Higher engagement often correlates with increased visibility on social platforms, as algorithms favor content that resonates with viewers.

Monitoring these metrics closely will enable content creators to refine their strategies and successfully increase shares.
