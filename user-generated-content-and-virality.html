User-Generated Content and Video Virality

User-generated content (UGC) creates authentic connections and enhances a video's virality through genuine viewer participation.

Encouraging viewers to share their stories or interpretations often leads to increased engagement and shares.

Brands that effectively leverage UGC can foster community involvement, significantly boosting their virality.
