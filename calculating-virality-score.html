How to Calculate Video Virality Score

Calculating a video's virality score involves analyzing several key performance indicators (KPIs). These include likes, shares, comments, and total views. By applying a weighting to each of these metrics, you can derive a score that reflects the video's potential to resonate with audiences.

Start with a basic framework that encompasses engagement metrics divided by the total reach. For example, if your video has 1000 views and 200 engagements, a simple formula could give you an engagement rate of 20%. This rate can be pivotal for predicting virality.

Once you establish a consistent method to calculate the virality score, use this data to compare different videos and adjust your future content accordingly. Over time, this process will enable you to identify patterns and refine your video production strategy for enhanced virality.
