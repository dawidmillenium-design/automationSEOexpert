Writing titles that survive truncation

Most surfaces show 40 to 60 characters before ellipsis, and mobile previews show fewer, so front-load the load-bearing words: put the subject and the tension first, qualifiers last. Compare How We Tested 12 Microphones for Street Interviews versus Street Interview Mic Test: The 12 Best Sounds — same information, but the second delivers its payload before truncation. Then check keyword alignment: the phrase someone would type to find your video should appear naturally in the first half. Finally, read your title aloud next to your opening line; redundancy between them wastes persuasion space, while complementary framing doubles it.

Title formulas, tested honestly

Formulas (number plus object plus outcome, question plus stakes, negation plus surprise) are starting hypotheses, not laws. Test them the scientific way: hold format and topic class constant, change only the title pattern, and compare click-through across at least six pairs before believing a winner. Beware the curiosity-gap arms race — clickbait titles that overpromise depress watch percentage, which drags the same distribution engine you were trying to trick. The durable optimum is the title that attracts exactly the viewers your video satisfies.
