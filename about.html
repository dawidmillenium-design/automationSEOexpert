About Video Virality Score

Video Virality Score is an independent research and education site dedicated to one question: why do some videos spread while others don't? We translate platform mechanics, audience psychology and performance data into practical guides for creators, marketers and studios.

Our mission

Most advice about "going viral" is folklore repeated between slides decks. We take the opposite approach: every claim we publish is grounded in observable metrics (hook rate, completion, share velocity), public platform documentation, or reproducible experiments we ran ourselves. When evidence is thin, we say so.

Who we are

The site is written by a small research team with backgrounds in social-video analytics, content strategy and data science:

  • Maya Chen — Head of Video Research. Ten years analysing short-form performance data at creator-analytics companies.
  • Daniel Okafor — Senior Content Strategist. Has consulted on more than 60 brand channels across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts since 2019.
  • Priya Raman — Data Analyst. Builds the scoring models behind our benchmarks; MSc Data Science, previously at a media-measurement agency.

Full credentials and how to reach each of them are on our author profile page.

How we work

Guides go through drafting, internal review against source material, and a quarterly refresh cycle. Recommendations are never paid for: we hold no affiliate relationships that influence our advice, accept no sponsored placements inside editorial content, and disclose any exception immediately.
