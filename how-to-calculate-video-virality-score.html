How to Calculate Video Virality Score

Calculating a video virality score involves analyzing several key metrics including views, engagement rate, and social shares.

By gathering data from platforms like YouTube and Facebook, you can create a formula that weights these metrics according to their influence on viral success.

This calculated score will guide your content creation strategy, enabling you to focus on elements that enhance viewer engagement and shareability.
