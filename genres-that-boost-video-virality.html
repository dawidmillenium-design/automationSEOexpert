Genres That Boost Video Virality

Certain video genres naturally garner higher virality scores due to their inherent entertainment value. Comedy, challenges, and DIY projects are among the most shared genres.

Research indicates that viewers have a propensity to engage with these formats due to their fun and informative nature. Therefore, incorporating these genres into your content can boost engagement.

Diversifying your content within these popular genres can help maintain audience interest and engagement, leading to increased virality scores.
