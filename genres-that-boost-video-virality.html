Genres That Boost Video Virality

Genres That Boost Video Virality Certain video genres naturally garner higher virality scores due to their inherent entertainment value. Comedy, challenges, and DIY projects are among the most shared genres.

Why it matters

Research indicates that viewers have a propensity to engage with these formats due to their fun and informative nature. Therefore, incorporating these genres into your content can boost engagement. Diversifying your content within these popular genres can help maintain audience interest and engagement, leading to increased virality scores. The Genre Benchmarks

Not all content types spread equally. The consistently high-performing genres share one trait: they give the sharer social currency. Ranked by typical share-rate strength:

  • Relatable comedy & POV sketches — "this is so me" is the single most common sharing motive.
  • Satisfying / process videos (cleaning, restoration, cooking, crafts) — near-universal appeal, language-free, loops well.
  • Emotional storytelling — kindness, reunions, comebacks; drives both shares and comments.
  • Educational micro-lessons — one concrete takeaway people save and forward ("you need to see this").
  • Challenges & duets — participatory formats that turn viewers into producers.
  • Behind-the-scenes & founder stories — the strongest genre for trust-building brands.

Choosing the Right Genre for Your Brand

Pick the intersection of three circles: what your audience already shares in your niche, what you can produce sustainably at quality, and what genuinely fits your brand voice. A B2B software company rarely wins with slapstick but often wins with fast screen-recorded "wait, that's possible?" demos. Test two genres per month for four posts each, then double down on whichever shows the best median completion and share rates — averages get skewed by one lucky hit, medians reveal your real floor.
