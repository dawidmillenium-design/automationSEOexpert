Psychological Triggers That Drive Video Virality

Certain psychological triggers, such as fear of missing out (FOMO) or curiosity, can compel viewers to watch and share videos.

Creating content that taps into these emotions fosters engagement and increases the likelihood of widespread sharing.

By leveraging psychological insights, creators can improve their chances of crafting videos that resonate deeply with audiences.
