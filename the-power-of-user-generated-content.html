The Power of User-Generated Content in Virality

User-generated content (UGC) can effectively boost a video's virality score. Content created by fans often resonates more deeply with viewers, fostering a sense of community.

Encouraging followers to create their own videos in response to a challenge or theme can lead to high engagement and sharing, increasing virality.

This strategy not only increases interaction but also decreases the production burden on creators, as they tap into their audience's creativity, ultimately enhancing virality.
