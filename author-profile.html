Meet the Authors

Every guide published on Video Virality Score is written by one of the three researchers below and reviewed by at least one other before it goes live. Their combined experience spans creator analytics, brand short-form strategy and measurement science.

Maya Chen — Head of Video Research

Maya leads the research agenda for the site. She spent more than a decade in social-video analytics, most recently as insights lead at a creator-analytics platform that tracks performance data across 40 million-plus videos per month. She sets the definitions we use for hook rate, completion and share velocity, and she signs off on every methodology claim in our guides.

Areas of expertise: retention curves, A/B testing of hooks and thumbnails, cross-platform benchmark design.

Contact: maya@example.com · Corrections and questions welcome.

Daniel Okafor — Senior Content Strategist

Daniel translates research into practice. Since 2019 he has consulted for more than 60 brand and creator channels across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, running first-hand experiments on formats, posting cadence and community mechanics. Most of our practical checklists originate from campaigns he has planned or audited.

Areas of expertise: short-form format design, creator collaborations, community-led distribution.

Contact: daniel@example.com

Priya Raman — Data Analyst

Priya builds and maintains the scoring models behind our benchmarks. She holds an MSc in Data Science and previously worked at a media-measurement agency, where she validated attention metrics against panel data. She is responsible for the statistical hygiene in our comparisons — sample sizes, confounders and confidence ranges.

Areas of expertise: composite metric design, engagement modelling, measurement validation.

Contact: priya@example.com

How we keep articles trustworthy

Authors disclose when guidance is based on client work, aggregated platform data or public documentation. All factual claims are checked against primary sources before publication, substantive corrections are logged openly, and no author accepts payment to recommend a product. The full rules live in our Editorial Policy & Methodology; the organisation behind the site is described on our About page.
