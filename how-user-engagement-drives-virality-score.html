How User Engagement Drives Virality Score

User engagement is the lifeblood of virality, as it directly influences the virality score. Metrics such as comments, likes, shares, and overall interaction act as indicators of content effectiveness.

A video that generates considerable engagement signals to platforms that it's worth sharing more widely. Content creators need to encourage dialogue and interaction to maximize these metrics.

By understanding and optimizing user engagement, creators can significantly increase their chances for videos to resonate with audiences and go viral.
